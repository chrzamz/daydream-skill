# Short idea examples

All examples below are synthetic. They are not drawn from private records and make no
claims about searches, existing products, demand, or tested outcomes. Each language pair
shows the same idea; normally respond only in the user's language. These are examples of
range, not categories to fill or a required output template.

## 1

**日常物件的虚构履历**
给普通物件设计一本角色档案：钥匙圈曾是灯塔守夜人，订书机是退休的桥梁工程师。
可以做成短漫画或收藏卡，用物件的真实结构长出它的职业、性格与关系，逐渐形成一个原创世界。

**Imaginary Biographies of Everyday Things**
Give ordinary objects character dossiers: a keyring was once a lighthouse keeper;
a stapler is a retired bridge engineer. Their physical shapes become the basis for
short comics or collectible cards that share an original fictional world.

## 2

**城市的影子导览**
一张按时段推荐街道阴影、树荫与有趣光影的散步地图，让人用另一种方式认识熟悉的城市。
它可以是一份摄影小刊、一组夏日路线，或一张随着时间变化的地图。

**A Guide to City Shadows**
A walking map organized around shade and interesting light at different times of day
could reveal a familiar city differently. It could take the form of a photography
zine, a set of summer routes, or a map that changes with the time of day.

## 3

**不必同时在线的桌游**
把一局合作桌游拆成每天一个可以邮寄、拍照或留言完成的回合，让不同作息的人一起经营一座虚构小岛。
每次行动留下一个小变化，下一位玩家可以隔天接着玩，规则围绕接力设计。

**A Board Game for Different Schedules**
Design a cooperative island-building game with one turn a day, delivered by postcard,
photo, or message. Each player leaves a small change for the next person to continue;
the rules make taking turns across days part of the game.

## 4

**修补痕迹图鉴**
把补丁、缝线、接缝与补色当作值得收藏的视觉语言，做一本普通人也能参与的修补图鉴。
每个条目把一种修补技法变成纹样，再延伸出小教程、印章或可打印的创作素材。

**A Field Guide to Visible Repairs**
Turn patches, stitches, joins, and touch-up paint into a visual vocabulary people can
collect and contribute to. Each entry translates a repair technique into a pattern,
with room to grow into a small tutorial, a stamp, or a printable creative resource.

## What the examples demonstrate

- Start with the idea itself and explain what makes it interesting or different.
- Keep the first pass to a title and one to three sentences. Leave demand analysis,
  implementation plans, and long source notes for a selected idea the user wants to explore.
- A connection can become fiction, a physical object, a publication, a service, a game,
  a product, or something that does not fit those forms. Do not make that list a taxonomy.
- Do not end every idea with a feelings question, a personal coaching prompt, or a teaser
  promising to reveal the idea later. Use an imagined experience preview only if requested.
- Add a brief public evidence sentence only when an inspected source supports it;
  synthetic examples and plausible associations are not evidence of market validation.
